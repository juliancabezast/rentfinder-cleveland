Section 8 Landlords in East Cleveland: Older Stock, Registration and Thin Payment Standards

East Cleveland is one of 17 suburbs where CMHA must collect a Certificate of Occupancy and/or Rental Registration with your RFTA packet, and CMHA pays a new contract only once it has the signed contracts, the executed lease and the occupancy permit. In ZIP 44112, CMHA's FY2026 two-bedroom payment standard is $1,220.

The document East Cleveland owners have to hand over

CMHA names the city outright. Its Leasing with CMHA page requires a copy of the Certificate of Occupancy and/or Rental Registration to be submitted with the Request for Tenancy Approval for units in 17 named suburbs: Bedford, Bedford Heights, Brook Park, Cleveland Heights, Cuyahoga Heights, East Cleveland, Euclid, Fairview Park, Garfield Heights, Lakewood, Lyndhurst, Maple Heights, Parma, Shaker Heights, South Euclid, University Heights and Warrensville Heights.

Read the mechanism, not just the list. This is not CMHA approving you: "CMHA does not have a pre-approval process for landlords or units," and its Administrative Plan adds that "The PHA does not formally approve an owner to participate in the HCV program." CMHA is simply declining to move a tenancy forward for a unit the city itself has not cleared.

For City of Cleveland units CMHA does the checking itself — "Effective October 1, 2025, CMHA will verify active City of Cleveland rental registrations through the City's Citizen Access Portal" (more here). In East Cleveland the paper is yours to produce, and municipal requirements vary: call the building department before you sign a lease, not after the RFTA is in.

It gates your first payment too

CMHA ties that document to the money. On a new contract, "you will be paid on the next scheduled payment date after HCVP has received three copies of the signed contracts, the executed lease and occupancy permit, when applicable." In East Cleveland, "when applicable" applies. Payments run "generally on the 3rd and 16th of each month," direct deposit is mandatory, and CMHA will not execute the contract without your IRS form W-9.

Execution has an outer limit — "no later than 60 calendar days from the beginning of the lease term," after which "[a]ny HAP contract executed after the 60-day period is void." Do not bridge a paperwork gap with early keys: CMHA warns that then "the tenant will be responsible for the full amount of the rent."

Older buildings are where the money is lost

Voucher units are still inspected under Housing Quality Standards, not NSPIRE: CMHA states that HUD moved the final transition deadline "from October 1, 2025 to February 1, 2027." An outside firm inspects against the 13 key housing quality aspects.

Two rules attach to pre-1978 construction. HUD Notice PIH 2024-26 REV-1 states that "any deteriorated paint in target housing... is considered a violation of HQS and the NSPIRE Standards," with 24 CFR Part 35 Subpart M applying where a child under age 6 resides or is expected to. Where defective paint is cited in such a unit, the owner gets 10 days from the initial move-in inspection — 30 from an annual — to produce a passed lead clearance test plus proof a certified lead abatement contractor did the work. Scoping, abatement and clearance inside ten days is the tightest deadline in the file.

That is not Cleveland's Lead Safe Certification, which covers City of Cleveland rentals only; East Cleveland is a separate municipality, and the federal rule above applies either way.

Then there is the list that fails a unit on the spot. CMHA's 24-hour emergency items include a missing or inoperable smoke or carbon monoxide detector, no water, no electricity, no heat between October 15 and April 15, a gas leak, sewer back-up, range burners that do not ignite, unlockable windows within six feet of ground level, and anything life-threatening. Since December 29, 2024 smoke detectors go one per level, one in each sleeping area and one within 21 feet of every bedroom door, battery units 10-year sealed or hardwired. Carbon monoxide detectors are required wherever there is a gas appliance, attached garage or wood-burning fireplace. More at What Gets a Unit Failed.

Fail twice and it stops being an inconvenience: "Rent is abated when the unit fails to pass on two consecutive inspections," starting the first of the following month, with the contract terminable after 30 days — and 24 CFR 982.4 defines abatement as "Stopping HAP payments to an owner with no potential for retroactive payment."

Payment standards in ZIP 44112 sit near the bottom of the county

CMHA sets payment standards per ZIP code under Small Area Fair Market Rents, publishing FY2026 figures effective January 1, 2026 for its 53 service-area ZIP codes. East Cleveland addresses sit mainly in ZIP 44112, but ZIP boundaries and city lines do not coincide — look your address up in CMHA's Rent Determination tool.

What sets your rent, and what CMHA does not stand behind

The real constraint is rent reasonableness: under 24 CFR 982.507(b) the comparison weighs location, quality, size, unit type and the age of the unit, plus amenities and utilities. CMHA's landlord page says it uses two comparable unassisted units, and on an initial contract the tenant's own portion cannot exceed 40% of the family's monthly income.

CMHA's own pitch promises "[s]table, guaranteed rent payments deposited directly into your account." That is CMHA's word, not ours — abatement is why, and CMHA states elsewhere that "HCVP does not pay for tenant caused damages." Screening stays yours. Nor is participation compulsory: the U.S. Commission on Civil Rights' Ohio Advisory Committee states that "Source-of-income discrimination is legal in most of Ohio," and PRRAC's March 2026 list of five Cuyahoga County municipalities with source-of-income bans does not include East Cleveland.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Certificate of Occupancy to rent to a voucher tenant in East Cleveland?
CMHA requires a copy of the Certificate of Occupancy and/or Rental Registration with the RFTA packet for units in 17 named suburbs, and East Cleveland is one of them. It is a municipal requirement CMHA enforces at the RFTA stage, not a CMHA approval — CMHA has no pre-approval process for landlords or units.
What is CMHA's payment standard in East Cleveland?
CMHA sets standards by ZIP code. In ZIP 44112 the FY2026 figures effective January 1, 2026 are $892 studio, $1,005 one-bedroom, $1,220 two-bedroom, $1,570 three-bedroom and $1,683 four-bedroom — under the $1,525 two-bedroom median across CMHA's 53 service-area ZIPs. It caps the subsidy, not your rent.
My East Cleveland house was built before 1978. What changes?
HUD treats deteriorated paint in target housing as a violation of both HQS and the NSPIRE standards. If defective paint is cited and a child under 6 will live there, you have 10 days from the initial move-in inspection — 30 from an annual — to provide a passed lead clearance test and proof a certified lead abatement contractor did the work.
Does Cleveland's Lead Safe Certification apply to my East Cleveland rental?
Lead Safe Certification is a City of Cleveland program covering City of Cleveland rentals built before 1978. East Cleveland is a separate municipality, and CMHA's utility allowance schedule lists the two cities separately. The federal lead rules under HQS apply to your unit regardless, and East Cleveland's own requirements come from its building department.
Is it legal to decline a voucher in East Cleveland?
The U.S. Commission on Civil Rights' Ohio Advisory Committee states that source-of-income discrimination is legal in most of Ohio. PRRAC's March 2026 compilation names five Cuyahoga County municipalities with source-of-income bans, and East Cleveland is not among them. Verify current local ordinance text before relying on that for a specific address.
